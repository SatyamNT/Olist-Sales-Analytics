Olist E-Commerce Sales Analytics

Where is revenue coming from, and where are deliveries hurting customers? · Built by Satyam Nath Tiwari

Data

Real Olist marketplace data: 96K delivered orders, Jan 2017 to Aug 2018 (Brazil). Amounts in Brazilian reais (R$).

Method

SQL joins across 6 tables, cleaning, KPI aggregation, then charts and written recommendations.

Cleaning

Delivered orders only; 2016 and late-2018 months dropped (too sparse); revenue = item price, excluding freight.

Tools

SQL (SQLite), Python, JavaScript, Chart.js.

Headline findings and recommendations (full dataset)

  • Growth: Jan–Aug 2018 revenue was +141% vs the same months of 2017 (R$7.22M vs R$2.99M). Revenue peaked in Nov 2017 (R$988K). Recommendation: plan stock and marketing for November.
  • Concentration: São Paulo (SP) generates 38.4% of revenue, and the top 3 states (SP, RJ, MG) about 63%. Recommendation: grow RJ and MG, and reduce dependence on one state.
  • Categories: health_beauty (9.3%), watches_gifts (8.8%) and bed_bath_table (7.8%) lead, with a long tail beyond the top 6. Recommendation: prioritise inventory and promotions here.
  • Delivery risk: 8.1% of orders arrive late, and late orders average 2.57 stars vs 4.30 for on-time orders. Recommendation: fix delivery estimates and carriers in the worst states (see state table).

    SQL used for the base dataset
    SELECT o.order_id, substr(o.order_purchase_timestamp,1,7) AS month,
       c.customer_state, t.product_category_name_english AS category,
       i.price,
       o.order_delivered_customer_date > o.order_estimated_delivery_date AS late,
       r.review_score
FROM orders o
JOIN order_items i ON i.order_id = o.order_id
JOIN products p    ON p.product_id = i.product_id
LEFT JOIN product_category_name_translation t
       ON t.product_category_name = p.product_category_name
JOIN customers c   ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id
LEFT JOIN (SELECT order_id, AVG(review_score) review_score
           FROM order_reviews GROUP BY order_id) r ON r.order_id = o.order_id
WHERE o.order_status = 'delivered'
  AND o.order_delivered_customer_date IS NOT NULL
  AND substr(o.order_purchase_timestamp,1,7) BETWEEN '2017-01' AND '2018-08';
